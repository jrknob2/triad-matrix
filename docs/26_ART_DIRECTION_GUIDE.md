# 26 - Drumcabulary Art Direction Guide

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the visual identity of Drumcabulary.

It exists to ensure that every screen, illustration, icon, banner, and promotional image shares the same visual language.

This guide intentionally separates:

• Application UI
• Artwork
• Branding

The UI presents information.

The artwork creates emotion.

Together they create the Drumcabulary experience.

---

# Brand Personality

Drumcabulary should immediately communicate:

• Professional
• Modern
• Confident
• Musical
• Premium
• Educational
• Energetic
• Focused

Never:

• Cartoon
• Comic book
• Heavy metal
• Childish
• Generic stock photography
• Cheap looking
• Busy

The feeling should be:

"A premium drum school in your pocket."

---

# Visual Identity

Overall style:

Modern studio photography meets premium mobile app.

Artwork should be:

• Semi-realistic
• High contrast
• Cinematic
• Dramatic lighting
• Clean composition
• Slightly stylized
• Premium product advertising quality

Imagine Apple advertising Ludwig drums.

---

# Hero Images

Every major screen begins with a hero.

Hero images establish mood.

They are NOT instructional.

They should inspire the student before they begin learning.

---

# Hero Composition

Every hero image follows the same layout.

Approximately:

70%

Visual Subject

30%

Negative Space

Example

+---------------------------------------------+

Subject

Negative Space

(UI)

+---------------------------------------------+

This reserved space is where Flutter places:

• titles
• descriptions
• chips
• buttons

Never place important visual elements inside the reserved UI area.

---

# Hero Image Zones

Each hero contains three layers.

Foreground

The subject

Examples

• snare
• practice pad
• drum kit
• drummer silhouette

Midground

Supporting atmosphere

Examples

• cymbals
• hardware
• smoke
• reflections

Background

Simple gradients

Soft lighting

Dark studio

Never clutter.

---

# Lighting

Lighting defines Drumcabulary.

Preferred

• directional lighting
• rim lighting
• stage lighting
• reflections
• subtle haze
• warm highlights

Avoid

• flat lighting
• daylight
• bright rooms
• white backgrounds

---

# Texture

Preferred materials

• maple
• chrome
• brushed aluminum
• matte black
• drum heads
• cymbals
• sticks

Everything should feel premium.

---

# Camera Style

Preferred

• drummer perspective
• three-quarter angle
• close detail
• shallow depth of field

Avoid

• straight side view
• overhead diagrams
• perfectly centered objects

---

# Motion

Motion should be subtle.

Examples

• stick blur
• cymbal vibration
• floating dust
• small sparks

Avoid comic-book effects.

# Artwork Reuse

Artwork belongs to Levels and Skills.

NOT individual lessons.

Example

Beginner

↓

Grooves

↓

Money Beat

↓

Crash Resolution

↓

Four Bar Groove

All reuse the Groove hero.

This keeps the app visually rich while requiring very little maintenance.

---

# Typography

Large lesson titles

Bold

High contrast

Minimal decoration

Section titles

Uppercase

Accent color

Body

Clean

Readable

No decorative fonts.

---

# Buttons

Buttons should feel physical.

Large.

Rounded.

High contrast.

Accent color used sparingly.

Avoid flat gray pills.

---

# Cards

Cards are the primary organizational element.

Rounded corners

Subtle shadow

Thin border

Dark surface

Generous padding

Cards should feel layered over the background.

---

# Icons

Use simple outline icons.

Consistent stroke width.

Avoid colorful icon packs.

The artwork provides personality.

Icons provide clarity.

---

# Illustration Rules

Do NOT place:

• text
• lesson names
• numbers
• logos

inside artwork.

Flutter owns all typography.

Artwork remains reusable.

---

# AI Generation Standard

Every generated image starts from the same base prompt.

Base Prompt

"Cinematic premium drum studio artwork, semi-realistic, professional educational app hero image, dramatic directional lighting, black studio background, realistic acoustic drum hardware, subtle smoke, orange rim lighting, shallow depth of field, premium product photography quality, clean composition, reserved negative space for mobile UI overlays, 16:9."

Only the subject changes.

This creates a unified visual language.

# Final Design Goal

A drummer should immediately recognize Drumcabulary from a single screenshot.

Without seeing the logo.

Without reading the text.

The visual language itself should communicate:

"This is where serious drummers learn."
